Empire: Epochs

Lead your people from the Stone Age to the Modern Age.

How to win: destroy the rival's Town Centers and citizens, or build a Wonder and defend it for 5 minutes.
Press F1 in game for controls.

Controls

Left-click select · drag box-select · Shift add

Double-click select all of that type on screen

Right-click move / attack / gather / build / repair / set rally point

A attack-move · S stop · Del delete

Ctrl+1-9 make group · 1-9 select group (tap twice to jump)

. next idle citizen · H Town Center · Space jump home

Arrows / screen edges scroll · Wheel zoom · P pause

Economy. Citizens gather food (berries, farms), wood, stone and gold and bring it to a Town Center or Storehouse. Houses raise your population limit.

Epochs. Advance at the Town Center. Every epoch unlocks buildings, upgrades all existing soldiers and boosts gathering.

Counters. Spears & guns beat cavalry · cavalry beats ranged & siege · ranged beats infantry · siege wrecks buildings.

Hotkeys for buildings and units are shown on each button.
